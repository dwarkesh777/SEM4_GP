Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how NestNode collects, uses, protects, and manages your information.

🛡
Our privacy commitment
NestNode is designed to help users discover and manage hostel and PG accommodation while respecting and protecting personal information.
📅 Effective Date: August 19, 2026 🔄 Last Updated: August 19, 2026

Welcome to NestNode (“we”, “our”, or “us”). NestNode is a hostel and PG booking platform that helps users discover accommodation options, view property information, and use booking-related services. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our website or mobile application.

1 Information We Collect

👤 Account Information

Name, email address, phone number, login details, and other information you provide while creating or managing an account.

🏠 Booking Information

Accommodation preferences, selected property details, booking requests, and information needed to provide booking-related services.

📱 Device & Usage Information

Basic technical information such as device type, app version, browser information, IP address, and interaction data used to improve reliability and security.

📍 Location Information

Location information may be used only when you provide permission or use location-based features to help show relevant accommodation options.

2 How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage your NestNode account.
  • Provide accommodation search, listing, and booking-related services.
  • Communicate about account activity, bookings, support requests, and important service updates.
  • Improve app performance, usability, features, and security.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, unauthorized access, and other harmful activity.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

3 How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only when reasonably necessary, including:

  • With accommodation providers or relevant parties to process a booking or service request.
  • With trusted service providers that support our infrastructure, hosting, communication, or technical operations.
  • When required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users and NestNode.
  • In connection with a business transfer, merger, or similar transaction, subject to applicable legal requirements.

4 Payments

If a payment feature is available, payment processing may be handled by a third-party payment service provider. NestNode does not intentionally store complete card details or other sensitive payment credentials unless explicitly required and handled through an appropriate secure payment system.

5 Permissions

The NestNode mobile application may request permissions only when required for a feature you choose to use. Depending on the functionality available in your version of the app, this may include:

  • Location: to help provide nearby or location-relevant accommodation results.
  • Camera or Photos: only if you choose a feature that requires selecting or uploading images.
  • Notifications: to send important service, booking, or account updates when enabled.

You can manage most permissions through your device settings.

6 Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. However, no internet or electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security.

7 Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain legitimate business records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and comply with legal obligations.

8 Your Choices and Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may be able to:

  • Access or update certain information in your account.
  • Request correction of inaccurate personal information.
  • Request deletion of your account or personal information, where legally applicable.
  • Withdraw optional permissions through your device settings.
  • Contact us with questions about how your information is handled.

9 Children's Privacy

NestNode is not intended for children below the minimum age required to use the service under applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

10 Third-Party Links and Services

Our platform may contain links to third-party websites, maps, payment services, or other services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, and we encourage you to review those policies before providing personal information.

11 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, technology, or legal requirements. The latest version will be posted with an updated “Last Updated” date.
